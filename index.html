A Flickersoft point-and-click adventure after Isaac Asimov’s story

  • Tap the floor to walk. Tap an object to look at it; doors take you through.
  • Pick a verb, then an object. Tap an inventory item to select it for Use or Give.
  • ◉ shows every hotspot. ? asks Donovan for a hint.
  • Play with or without sound. About 5–10 minutes.
